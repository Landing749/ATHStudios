01

Installing it

Four steps, first-run only — after that it just opens.

1

Download the file

Click the download button above. Your browser saves InkResetTool.exe to your Downloads folder.

2

Allow it through your browser's check

Chrome or Edge may flag the file as unrecognized since it isn't signed with a paid certificate. Choose Keep or Keep anyway when prompted.

3

Get past Windows SmartScreen

Windows will likely show a blue "protected your PC" screen the first time you run it. This is expected — see exactly what to click below.

4

Connect your printer and run

Make sure your printer is connected via USB and powered on, then launch InkResetTool.exe. Select your model and follow the on-screen steps.

02

About the SmartScreen warning

You'll very likely see this — here's exactly what it means and what to click.

Windows protected your PC✕

Windows protected your PC

Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting. Running this app might put your PC at risk.

App: InkResetTool.exe  ·  Publisher: Unknown publisher
More info

It's not a virus warning. It just means the .exe hasn't been signed with a paid Microsoft-verified certificate — something every small, independently distributed Windows tool runs into.

  • 1 Click More info in the blue window.
  • 2 A Run anyway button appears at the bottom — click it.
  • 3 InkResetTool opens normally. This only happens the first time you run this specific file.
Antivirus flags: some antivirus tools may also flag it, for the same unsigned-publisher reason, not because it contains malware. If you'd rather verify first, scan the file on VirusTotal before running it.
